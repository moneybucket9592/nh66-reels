Privacy Policy

NH Reels Publisher · Last updated: 24 November 2026

NH Reels Publisher is a small private tool run by the owner of the Mallus Jilla Facebook Page and the @mallus_jilla Instagram account. It makes short road-safety videos about published Indian road regulations and posts them to those two accounts. It is not an official government, police or Motor Vehicles Department service.

What the app does

  • Posts videos and captions to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • Reads basic details of those two accounts (name, ID, posting limits) so it can post to them.

What information we collect

None from the public. The app does not offer a login to anyone else, and it does not collect, store or share any personal information about people who watch, like or comment on our posts.

The only data the app keeps is the owner's own access keys for the owner's own accounts. They are stored privately on the owner's computer and are never shared or sold.

Cookies and tracking

This page and the app use no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no data about anyone other than the owner, there is nothing to delete for other users. If you believe the app holds any data about you, send a message to the Mallus Jilla Facebook Page and it will be checked and deleted within 30 days. You can also remove the app's access to your own Facebook account at any time under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to the Mallus Jilla Facebook Page.
